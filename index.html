Seeking a Summer 2027 internship: battery modeling, BMS algorithms, controls

Farzaneh Barat

Ph.D. Student in Mechanical Engineering · University of Kansas

My research develops uncertainty-aware estimation, identification, and control methods for lithium-ion batteries. I combine physics-based models with ensemble Bayesian inference and machine learning to infer what sensors cannot measure directly (state of charge, model parameters, and state of health) and to quantify how much each estimate can be trusted.

Current focus

  • Online parameter identification: sequential Ensemble Kalman Inversion for nonlinear stochastic state-space models.
  • Robust state-of-charge estimation: physics-based observers augmented with neural ODEs to compensate for model error.
  • Thermal management & smart charging: model-based control on coupled electro-thermal models to limit temperature nonuniformity and degradation.
  • Second-life batteries: laboratory characterization of retired modules (capacity, HPPC, dQ/dV, dynamic cycling) to assess reuse potential.

Research

Neural-Network-Enhanced SOC Observer

Robust state-of-charge estimation that pairs a nominal nonlinear double-capacitor (NDC) Luenberger observer with a neural ODE that learns the SOC residual from sparse labels.

Neural ODEObserver designSOC

Recursive Parameter Identification of Nonlinear Stochastic State-Space Models

A sequentialized Ensemble Kalman Inversion (SEnKI) framework for online identification with latent states, combining a local particle filter with Kalman-type parameter updates. Validated on an NDC battery model and a nonlinear benchmark against augmented and dual EKF/UKF.

Sequential EnKIParticle filteringOnline ID

Parameter Identification of Battery Models

Uncertainty-aware calibration of equivalent-circuit and nonlinear battery state-space models from voltage and temperature measurements using Ensemble Kalman Inversion.

EnKIECMsElectro-thermal

Thermal Management, Smart Charging & Second-Life Batteries

2026 – Present

Developing model-based thermal-management and smart-charging control on coupled electro-thermal models to regulate temperature, reduce nonuniformity, and mitigate degradation in cylindrical and pouch cells. Characterizing retired modules (capacity, HPPC, dynamic cycling, thermal) to evaluate degradation and support physics-informed modeling and diagnostics for energy-storage reuse.

Health Diagnostics & SOH-Aware Modeling

Extracting degradation features and building state-of-health-aware models for reliable health estimation in demanding applications such as eVTOL systems.

Probabilistic Solver for PMP-Based Optimal Control

Solving continuous-time optimal control problems from Pontryagin's Minimum Principle as boundary-value problems via probabilistic estimation and smoothing, with built-in uncertainty quantification.

Talks

Neural SDE: Stabilizing Neural ODE Networks with Stochastic Noise

Paper presentation · University of Kansas · 2026

How adding a learned diffusion term to neural ODEs acts as stochastic regularization, the stability theory behind it (stochastic and almost-sure exponential stability under Lipschitz drift), gradient computation through SDE solvers, and the resulting gains in generalization and robustness to input perturbations.

Paper: X. Liu, T. Xiao, S. Si, Q. Cao, S. Kumar, and C.-J. Hsieh, arXiv:1906.02355, 2019.

Latent Matters: Learning Deep State-Space Models

Paper presentation · University of Kansas · 2026

Limitations of standard ELBO training for dynamics learning, the constrained-optimization alternative, hierarchical priors for latent-state initialization, and the Extended Kalman VAE, with connections to Kalman filtering, smoothing, and system identification.
